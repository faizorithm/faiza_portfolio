Hi, I'm Faiza.

I'm a Computer Science student at CUNY Hunter College, graduating in May 2027.

I'm drawn to the intersection of technology, creativity, and human experience. My interests span artificial intelligence, data science, software development, and design, but what connects them is a desire to understand how things work, question how they could work better, and turn ideas into something meaningful.

Whether I'm exploring patterns in data, developing applications, or experimenting with design, I approach each project with curiosity and a willingness to learn. I'm especially interested in how emerging technologies can be designed thoughtfully to solve real problems and improve the way people interact with the world around them.

For me, computer science isn't just about writing code. It's about understanding possibilities and building toward them.
